# Megu Trademark & Brand Guidelines

This document explains how you may use the **Megu** name, the Megu voxel cube logo (`website/logo.svg`), and related marks (`Megu Official`, `Megu Certified Host`, `Megu Cloud`).

> **Open-source code freedom vs. trademark clarity:**  
> The Megu (`megu`) source code is licensed under [`LGPL-3.0-or-later`](COPYING.LESSER). Nothing in this policy restricts your rights under `LGPL-3.0-or-later` to use, study, modify, self-host, or redistribute the software. Trademark rules exist solely to prevent confusion about what is official and to protect users from misleading or unauthorized commercial offerings.

---

## 1. Uses permitted without prior permission

You are free to use the **Megu** name and logo without asking when:

- **Personal & community use:** Running Megu for yourself, friends, or a non-commercial community server, and stating truthfully that your server runs Megu (e.g., *"Powered by Megu"* or *"Compatible with Megu"*).
- **Mod & shader authors:** Stating that your mod, modpack, resource pack, or shaderpack works with Megu (e.g., *"Made for Megu 1.21.1"* or *"Tested on Megu"*).
- **Editorial, videos, benchmarks & education:** Referring to Megu accurately in articles, YouTube/streaming videos, benchmarks, academic papers, or tutorials.
- **Unmodified redistribution:** Redistributing unmodified official release binaries or source archives under `LGPL-3.0-or-later` while preserving copyright and license notices.

## 2. Uses that require written permission or a partner agreement

You must obtain prior written permission (or a signed partner/sponsor agreement) before:

- **Commercial hosting & managed services:** Marketing or selling paid Minecraft hosting plans using *"Megu Official"*, *"Megu Certified Host"*, *"Megu Cloud"*, or presenting a hosting service as endorsed by or affiliated with the Megu project.
  - Note: A host may factually list *"Supports Megu (megu)"* in a plain technical feature list alongside Paper/NeoForge/Fabric, provided it does not imply official endorsement, partnership, or certification.
- **Modified forks & derivative distributions:** Distributing modified builds of Megu under the name *"Megu"* or with the Megu logo in a way that suggests they are official builds. Forks are welcome under `LGPL-3.0-or-later`, but must use a distinct name and logo (e.g., *"MyEngine, based on Megu (megu)"*).
- **Domains, packages & accounts:** Registering domains, social media handles, or package/storefront names intended to impersonate the official Megu project.
