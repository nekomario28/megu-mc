`block_predicate_filter(would_survive, oak_sapling)`. Dirt supports the
sapling; stone does not, so the port skips the tree and moves to the next
count position. Tree origin sets for the fixture are 50 reference / 51 port
(41 shared, 9/10 one-sided), but the first divergence is the support block,
not tree RNG, trunk placement, dirt placement, or fancy attachments.

terrain/surface residual. The target chunk's tree origin/dirt/fancy checks
remain at 0.
