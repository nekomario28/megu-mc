# SpringFeature above/below orientation

The target's remaining 20 lava cells are a flowing lava pool whose source is
(-1024,-16,512). The source cell itself was missing in the port; the other 20
are lava spread written by the saved world's runtime, not worldgen.

`SpringFeature` (`edp`) tests `jd.d()` = **above** and, when
`requires_block_below`, `jd.e()` = **below**; its rock/hole counts also use the
four horizontals plus **below**. The port had the opposite pair (below/below2,
and above in the count). After the fix the port writes the source at
(-1024,-16,512) exactly like the reference.

No-tick target residual remains 53: 20 flowing lava (runtime), ~30 missing ore
cells, 1 chest, 1 grass. This is the next layer.
