# Fancy branch free-cell predicate

`TrunkPlacer.isFree` (`ehm.b`) is `validTreePos || BlockTags.LOGS`.
`validTreePos` (`edq.c`) is air or `REPLACEABLE_BY_TREES` (mapping `awe.co`);
`BlockTags.LOGS` is `awe.u`. Leaves are already in `REPLACEABLE_BY_TREES`, so
both leaves and logs are free; stone is not.

The branch check now names that predicate in one helper and pins all four cases.
The 5x5 tree-on diff stays 72 (the remaining branch/canopy/leaf cells), so this
refactor is not the 72-cell cause; it prevents a future tag swap from silently
turning the branch into a stone-only path.
