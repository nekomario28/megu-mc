# Code of Conduct / 行動規範

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Megu (`megu-mc`) community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Focusing on technical facts, reproducible test cases, and constructive feedback
* Respecting clean-room provenance rules and third-party intellectual property
* Accepting constructive criticism gracefully and keeping discussions on-topic
* Showing empathy and courtesy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
* Sharing or requesting pirated software, decompiled Mojang source code, or proprietary/ARR code
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via GitHub or `partners@megumin.tech`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## 日本語要約（行動規範）

Megu（`megu-mc`）コミュニティは、誰もが敬意をもって技術的な議論・貢献を行えるオープンな場であることを目指します。

- **歓迎される行動**: 再現可能な事実やテストに基づく建設的な議論、クリーンルーム開発原則および第三者の権利・ライセンスの尊重、他者への敬意と配慮。
- **禁止される行動**: 誹謗中傷・ハラスメント・個人攻撃、Mojang の逆コンパイルコードや不適切な非公開資産・海賊版の共有・要求、個人情報の晒し行為、その他コミュニティの健全性を損なう行為。
- **報告・対応**: 問題のある言動を確認した場合、メンテナは警告・投稿削除・ブロック等の措置を行うことがあります。連絡は GitHub または `partners@megumin.tech` までお願いします。
